# Week 1 & 2: kinematics, forces & Newton's laws, work & energy, friction

Welcome to the first two weeks of the course. These weeks lay the groundwork for the rest of
engineering mechanics: how we describe motion (**kinematics**), what causes it (**forces** and
**Newton's laws**), the bookkeeping of **work and energy**, and the ever-present complication of
**friction**.

Each section below pairs a short problem statement with an interactive animation you can play
with directly in the browser — change the parameters, press **Play**, and watch how the physics
responds. Work through them in order:

1. {ref}`Parabolic flight <w1-parabolic-flight>` — projectile motion under gravity.
2. {ref}`Motion down a slope <w1-motion-down-a-slope>` — sliding on an incline.
3. {ref}`Killer loop <w1-killer-loop>` — energy conservation around a vertical loop.
4. {ref}`Spring-loaded gun <w1-spring-loaded-gun>` — elastic potential energy launching a mass.
5. {ref}`Spring–slope system <w1-spring-slope-system>` — combining springs, slopes and friction.

```{tip}
The animations are self-contained (plain JavaScript + Plotly) — they run instantly, with no
need to start the {fa}`rocket` Live Code kernel.
```
