# Week 3: systems of particles, rigid bodies – part 1 (kinematics, angular momentum, torques)

This week we move from single particles to **systems of particles** and the first ideas of
**rigid-body** motion. The key concepts are the **centre of mass**, **conservation of momentum**,
and — as we begin to rotate things — **angular momentum** and **torques**.

Work through the three examples in order:

1. {ref}`Exploding projectile <w3-exploding-projectile>` — one body splits into two, momentum shared.
2. {ref}`Ballistic pendulum <w3-ballistic-pendulum>` — an inelastic collision followed by an energy-conserving swing.
3. {ref}`1D elastic collisions <w3-elastic-collision>` — head-on collisions where both momentum and kinetic energy are conserved.

```{tip}
Watch the conserved quantities in each readout: total momentum $P$ never changes, while kinetic
energy changes only when the interaction is inelastic.
```
