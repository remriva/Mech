# Week 4: rigid bodies – part 2 (rotational inertia and energy, equations of motion), gravity

Building on the kinematics of rotation, this week is about the **dynamics** of rigid bodies:
**rotational inertia**, **rotational kinetic energy** and the rotational equation of motion. We finish with
**gravity**, using a swinging pendulum to measure $g$.

Work through the four examples in order:

1. {ref}`Rolling motion down a slope <w4-rolling-motion>` — why a sphere beats a cylinder beats a hoop.
2. {ref}`Weight hanging with a pulley <w4-weight-pulley>` — how a *massive* pulley changes the motion.
3. {ref}`Maxwell's wheel <w4-maxwells-wheel>` — energy traded between falling and spinning.
4. {ref}`Measuring gravity with a swing pendulum <w4-measuring-gravity>` — infer $g$ from the period.

```{tip}
Notice how the moment of inertia $I$ controls everything here: a larger $I$ (mass farther from
the axis) means slower acceleration but more energy stored in rotation.
```
